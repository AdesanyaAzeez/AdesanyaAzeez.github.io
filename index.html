ABOUT ME

Turning raw data into business value, one insight at a time.

Hi, I’m AbdulAzeez Adesanya, an Analytics Engineer with 4+ years of experience building reliable, scalable data systems that turn raw operational data into structured, analytics-ready datasets.

My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, analytics engineering, and business intelligence. I design data pipelines, analytical data models, and reporting layers that make complex, multi-source data reliable, performant, and easy to consume. I work across the data lifecycle — from ingestion and transformation to warehousing, modelling, optimisation, and BI.

I have hands-on experience working with ClickHouse, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Python, Airbyte, and modern BI platforms. My focus is not simply moving data from one system to another, but designing data architectures that are efficient, maintainable, and fit for analytical workloads. This includes developing reusable data models, optimising complex SQL queries, designing incremental data pipelines, and improving the performance and reliability of analytical workloads.

Performance and data quality are central to how I approach engineering. I work with large datasets and production workloads where query performance, data consistency, schema management, and operational reliability matter. I also design validation and reconciliation processes to ensure that the data powering reports, dashboards, and business decisions remains accurate and trustworthy.

Coming from a strong data analysis background, I also understand the business context behind the data. I build analytical solutions with the end user in mind — ensuring that datasets are not only technically sound, but structured around the questions the business actually needs to answer.

I enjoy solving problems where data architecture, performance, and business requirements intersect — whether that means designing an analytical warehouse, engineering an ingestion pipeline, optimising a complex query, modelling a reporting layer, or turning fragmented operational data into a reliable source of truth.
